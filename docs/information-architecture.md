# IBAA Information Architecture

The rebuilt site will present IBAA as a national, non-sectarian Buddhist leadership organization while giving visitors short, predictable paths to organizational information, programs, current activity, member communities, membership, and giving. The public navigation will group related routes to avoid placing thirteen equal-weight links in a single row.

| Navigation group | Public routes | Primary purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About | `/about`, `/about/history`, `/leadership`, `/annual-delegates` | Explain IBAA’s history, mission, governance, and national representation. |
| Programs | `/programs`, `/study-circles`, `/programs/youth`, `/programs/interfaith`, `/programs/pilgrimages` | Present ongoing work and create clear participation paths. |
| Community | `/member-temples`, `/events` | Help visitors find communities and attend activities. |
| News & Media | `/news-media`, `/news/:slug`, `/resources`, `/media/:id-:slug` | Preserve the publication archive, external press, documents, photography, and future videos. |
| Participate | `/join`, `/donate`, `/contact` | Convert interest into membership, temple participation, donations, and contact. |
| Administration | `/admin`, `/admin/content`, `/admin/media` | Provide authenticated, role-gated publishing and asset management. These routes are excluded from indexing. |

The homepage will use a deliberate editorial sequence rather than a generic grid of cards. It will open with **“Uniting America’s Buddhist Traditions”**, establish national credibility through a White House Vesak feature, explain the three-tradition umbrella, highlight Dharma Study Circles, provide current news and events, introduce member communities, and finish with membership and donation calls to action.

| Homepage section | Content behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Real White House or representative IBAA photography, concise mission statement, and primary links to About and Join. |
| National leadership | White House Vesak and national delegation context with verified captions and dates. |
| Three traditions | Equal, non-hierarchical presentation of Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana under IBAA’s non-sectarian mission. |
| Study Circles | Latest meeting image, location and date context, program explanation, and contact path. |
| News & events | Published database entries with real dates, categories, and featured images. |
| Member communities | Search prompt and tradition filters leading to the directory. |
| Participate | Membership, temple participation, donation, and contact actions. |

## Content Presentation Rules

Every migrated article will visibly preserve its original publication date, author when available, categories, featured image, captions, credits, downloadable documents, and a short **Legacy archive** note linking the original path context. Dates are not rewritten to imply recency. Governance counts, officer roles, and contact details found in legacy text must be editorially reverified before being labeled current.

Media entries may contain an image gallery, document card, external press link, or validated YouTube embed. Articles remain useful without video; meeting minutes and written summaries are the preferred record for recurring Dharma meetings, while YouTube embeds support special events and archival recordings.
