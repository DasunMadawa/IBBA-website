# IBAA Visual Direction

The selected direction is **Civic Buddhist Archive**: a national inter-tradition institution rendered with warm archival-paper grounds, deep forest, oxblood maroon, disciplined saffron-gold accents, literary serif typography, verified documentary photography, and restrained temple-derived linework.

The interface should feel calm, ceremonial, and public-minded rather than commercial. Its signature motif is a set of three balanced circles joined by a shared center, representing IBAA’s practical Theravada–Mahayana–Vajrayana umbrella while the content explains the far greater diversity within and across those categories. The motif is not presented as a sacred emblem or a replacement for any tradition’s own symbols.

The organization’s existing emblem and wordmark remain part of the identity, but layouts should integrate them into a disciplined institutional lockup. Inner pages should resemble a living archive through captions, dates, source notes, timelines, and record markers rather than generic marketing cards.
